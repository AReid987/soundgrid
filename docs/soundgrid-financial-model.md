# SoundGrid Financial Model v1

**Prepared:** February 24, 2026
**Author:** Fiona "Flux" Rivera — Strategic Finance & Operations
**Project:** SoundGrid — Music Industry B2B/B2C Marketplace Platform

---

## Executive Summary

SoundGrid's financial model is built on a tiered SaaS subscription base supplemented by transaction fee revenue from contract execution, sync licensing placements, and live booking. The model targets $1.2M ARR at end of Year 1, $6.8M ARR at end of Year 2, and $22.4M ARR at end of Year 3 — positioning the company for a Series A raise at the 18–24 month mark. Unit economics are designed around a target LTV:CAC ratio of 4.5:1, consistent with top-quartile B2B SaaS benchmarks, and a 14-month blended CAC payback period.

---

## 1. Revenue Model Architecture

### 1.1 Revenue Streams

SoundGrid operates three revenue layers:

**Layer 1: SaaS Subscriptions (Recurring)**
Monthly subscription tiers for individual artists, producers, and teams. This is the predictable base.

**Layer 2: Transaction Fees (Variable)**
Percentage fee on contract execution, sync licensing placements, and live booking payments processed through the platform. This scales with GMV.

**Layer 3: Enterprise / B2B Seats (Recurring)**
Annual contracts for music publishers, management companies, and entertainment law firms accessing the platform for client portfolio management.

#### Transaction Fees

| Transaction Type            | Fee Structure          | Rationale                                              |
| :-------------------------- | :--------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------- |
| Contract Execution (escrow) | 2.5% of contract value | Platform-facilitated payment protection                |

| Live Booking (via platform) | 8% of booking fee      | Booking facilitation; below traditional agent (15–20%) |
| Beat / Sample Licensing     | 10% of license fee     | Direct marketplace transaction                         |
