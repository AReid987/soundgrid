Agreement ID: NEX-2024-EV01-SEC-PRO

ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS & INTERPRETATION

Clause 1.1: Grant of Rights. The Artist hereby grants to the Company the exclusive right to represent the Artist as their sole and exclusive professional manager throughout the world in all areas of the entertainment industry, including but not limited to music, motion pictures, television, and digital media.

Clause 1.2: Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for an initial period of twenty-four (24) months, unless terminated earlier in accordance with the provisions herein.

ARTICLE 2: COMMISSION & REVENUE

Clause 2.1: Management Fee. In consideration for the services to be rendered by the Company, Artist shall pay the Company a sum equal to fifteen percent (15%) of all Gross Income earned by Artist during the Term from Artist's professional activities.
