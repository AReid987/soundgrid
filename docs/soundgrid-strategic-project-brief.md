## 1. Vision Statement

**SoundGrid is the operating layer for independent music professionals** — a unified platform where artists, producers, music supervisors, and venue operators contract, license, book, and get paid without friction, delay, or exploitation.

The music industry generates $36B+ annually, yet independent creators — the majority of active music professionals — lack the basic commercial infrastructure that every other knowledge worker takes for granted. Contracts go unsigned for months after album releases. Sync deals fall through due to metadata gaps. Touring artists manage bookings via email and verbal agreements. SoundGrid ends this.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

### 2.1 The Core Problem

Independent music professionals operate in a system designed for major labels, not creators. The result is a structural breakdown across three critical workflows:

**Workflow 1: Contracts & Payments**

- Music is routinely released before producer agreements are signed (Billboard-documented: Travis Scott *Utopia*, Kanye West *Vultures*)

- No platform prevents distribution until contracts are executed

- Split sheets are informal or verbal, creating post-release ownership disputes

- No escrow mechanism exists for independent artist advance payments

- Producers — the most financially vulnerable segment — have no dedicated tooling

**Workflow 2: Sync Licensing**

- Sync licensing is an $800M+ market growing at 7.2% CAGR, yet independent artists capture a fraction of available placements

- Music supervisors spend disproportionate time on clearance due to metadata gaps and unclear ownership chains

- No platform provides the artist-side workflow: submission → negotiation → custom license generation → royalty tracking

- Existing platforms (Musicbed, Artlist, Epidemic Sound) serve buyers exclusively; artists have no equivalent infrastructure

**Workflow 3: Live Music & Touring**

- The global live music market is $31.1B, growing at 9.6% CAGR

- Independent and mid-tier touring artists (earning $50K–$500K from live) manage routing, booking, and payments via email and spreadsheets

- No platform integrates routing → venue discovery → booking → contract → payment for this segment

- Venue operators lack standardized contracts and dispute resolution for independent bookings

### 2.2 The Cost of Inaction

- Producers lose weeks or months of delayed payment per release cycle

- Artists lose sync placement revenue to competitors with cleaner metadata

- Independent venues face payment disputes and legal exposure from informal agreements

- The industry collectively loses hundreds of millions annually to friction, disputes, and lost opportunities

## 3. Solution Overview

SoundGrid is a **B2B/B2C SaaS marketplace platform** with three integrated product pillars:

### Pillar 1: ContractGrid

**The contract-to-payment engine for music professionals**

- AI-assisted contract creation (producer agreements, split sheets, sync licenses, performance agreements, management contracts, beat licenses)

- Real-time negotiation workflow with version history and e-signature

- Escrow-backed payment execution tied to contract milestones

- Royalty calculation and automated split distribution

- Integration with major DAWs (Ableton, Logic, Pro Tools) and DSPs (DistroKid, TuneCore, Spotify for Artists)

- Pre-release verification gate: prevents distribution unlock until agreements are executed

### Pillar 2: SyncGrid

**The sync licensing marketplace for independent artists and supervisors**

- Artist-facing: catalog submission, metadata management, custom license generation, deal negotiation

- Supervisor-facing: curated discovery, cleared music search, direct outreach, rapid clearance workflow

- Multi-territory compliance automation

- 15% platform fee on placements; subscription for enhanced discovery placement

- Integration with Content ID systems (HAAWK, Identifyy) for downstream protection

### Pillar 3: LiveGrid

**The touring and live music coordination platform**

- Tour routing optimizer (geography + availability + venue capacity matching)

- Venue discovery marketplace with standardized booking contracts

- Performance agreement generation and escrow-backed payment

- Fan CRM and show promotion tools

- Revenue tracking across all live income streams

- 8% platform fee on bookings processed through the platform

## 7. Competitive Positioning

### 7.1 Positioning Statement

*"For independent music professionals who are tired of getting paid late and losing deals to broken infrastructure, SoundGrid is the all-in-one commercial operating system that connects contracts, sync licensing, and live booking into one seamless workflow — unlike fragmented point solutions that serve only one part of the problem."*

### 7.2 Competitive Moats

1. **Network effects:** Two-sided marketplace dynamics — more artists attract more supervisors and venues; more demand attracts more supply

2. **Data moat:** Proprietary metadata, contract templates, and pricing benchmarks improve with scale

3. **Switching costs:** Once artists manage catalog, contracts, and bookings in one platform, migration cost is extremely high

4. **Compliance layer:** Multi-territory licensing compliance automation is technically complex to replicate

5. **Escrow trust:** Payment execution tied to contracts creates trust that no competitor currently offers

### 7.3 Differentiation Matrix

| Competitor   | Their Strength             | Their Gap                | SoundGrid Advantage                       |
| :----------- | :------------------------- | :----------------------- | :---------------------------------------- |
| Flou         | Simple contracts           | No payment, no workflow  | Full stack: create → negotiate → pay      |
| Artlist      | Sync distribution at scale | No artist-side tooling   | Artist infrastructure + buyer marketplace |
| Sofar Sounds | Live event community       | No contracts, no routing | Touring workflow end-to-end               |
| Bandsintown  | Tour discovery             | No booking or contracts  | Complete booking → contract → payment     |
| Clearnote    | AI contract drafting       | Lawyer-facing, expensive | Artist-priced, workflow-integrated        |

## 8. Product Strategy

### 8.1 Build Sequence (MVP → V1 → V2)

**MVP (Months 1–4): ContractGrid Core**

- Contract creation wizard (producer agreements, split sheets, performance agreements)

- E-signature integration (DocuSign/HelloSign API)

- Basic escrow payment (Stripe Connect)

- User authentication and profile management

- Free + Creator tier only

**V1 (Months 5–9): ContractGrid Full + SyncGrid Beta**

- Full contract template library (all music contract types)

- Negotiation workflow with version history

- Royalty tracking dashboard

- SyncGrid: artist catalog submission + supervisor discovery portal

- Professional + Collective tiers

- Mobile-responsive web application

**V2 (Months 10–18): LiveGrid + Enterprise + Marketplace**

- LiveGrid: venue discovery, tour routing, booking contracts

- B2B enterprise tier launch (Publisher Pro, Management Suite)

- Sync marketplace with AI-powered music matching

- API integrations: DistroKid, TuneCore, Spotify for Artists, DAW plugins

- SOC 2 Type II certification

- International expansion (UK, Canada)

### 8.2 Key Product Principles

1. **Creator-first pricing:** Every feature accessible at a price point independent creators can afford

2. **Workflow integration:** Every action (contract, payment, booking) connects to the next without leaving the platform

3. **Escrow as trust:** Payment execution backed by escrow is the trust mechanism that drives adoption and retention

4. **Metadata as infrastructure:** Clean, consistent metadata across catalog, contracts, and bookings is the platform's core data asset

5. **AI as assistant, not replacement:** AI accelerates contract creation and sync matching but keeps humans in control of all deal decisions

### 9.1 Phase 1 GTM (Months 1–6): Community-Led Growth

- **Channel 1 — Producer Communities:** Target Beat Stars, Reddit (r/WeAreTheMusicMakers, r/beatmakers), Discord servers. CAC: ~$85

- Conference presence: SXSW, A3C, NAMM, Music Biz

### 9.3 Phase 3 GTM (Months 19–36): Enterprise + International

- Direct enterprise sales team for publisher and management company accounts

- International expansion via local music industry partnerships

- API ecosystem for integration partners (DAWs, DSPs, entertainment law platforms)

## 13. Project Constraints & Assumptions

### Constraints

- Initial build must be achievable by a 4-engineer team in 4 months (MVP scope)

- Must be SOC 2 Type II compliant before enterprise sales (target Month 12)

- Escrow functionality requires Stripe Treasury or equivalent — no self-managed fund custody

- Contract AI must use human-reviewed template library — no fully autonomous contract generation without review

- All payment processing must comply with FinCEN/MSB regulations for money transmission

### Assumptions

- DocuSign or HelloSign API is available at startup pricing ($0.50–$1.50/envelope)

- Stripe Connect supports marketplace escrow for music industry use cases

- Target users have smartphones and web access; mobile-first responsive web is sufficient for MVP (native app in V2)

- Entertainment attorney partnerships can be secured through music industry network before launch

- Producer/artist communities on Discord, Reddit, and BeatStars are reachable via organic community strategy
